# PADMA

PADMA is a personal astrological diary designed for practitioners of Jyotish (Vedic astrology).

The application provides detailed analysis of planetary transits, nakshatras, yogas, and other traditional calculations.

📅 Daily insights  
View detailed daily time segments with clear visual representation.

🌙 Transit analysis  
Analyze planetary influences based on Moon and Lagna positions.

🎨 Visual calendar  
Color-coded calendar helps you quickly understand time quality.

📝 Notes and events  
Add your own events and reminders.

⚙️ Flexible settings  
Customize calculations and appearance.

🔒 Privacy  
All data is stored locally on your device.

❤️ Support  
You can support development voluntarily.

PADMA provides astrological calculations for personal analysis and practice.  
It does not guarantee outcomes.
