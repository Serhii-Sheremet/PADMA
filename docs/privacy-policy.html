PADMA – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 April 2026

1. Introduction

PADMA (Personal Astrological Diary Mobile App) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the application.

2. Information We Collect

The application may collect limited information in the following cases:

  • Email address (optional) — only if you provide it when submitting feedback or questions
  • User-entered data — such as notes, events, and preferences (stored locally on your device)

3. How We Use Information

We use the information only for:

  • Responding to user questions or feedback (if email is provided)
  • Improving the application

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

4. Data Storage

Most data is stored locally on your device. We do not maintain a remote database of your personal data.

5. Notifications

The application may use local notifications to remind you about your own events. These notifications are generated on your device and do not involve external servers.

6. Third-Party Services

The application may use standard platform services provided by Google Play Services (Android) and Apple system services (iOS). These services may collect anonymous technical data according to their own privacy policies.

7. Disclaimer

This application is intended for informational and personal use only. It does not provide professional, medical, financial, or legal advice.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us via the feedback form in the application.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
