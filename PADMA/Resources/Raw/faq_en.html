1. General Information
What is PADma?

PADMA is an abbreviated name of the application: "Personal Astrological Diary (mobile application)".

Who is this application intended for?

PADma is a personal astrological diary designed for practitioners of Vedic astrology.

What is the purpose of this application?

The application helps analyze planetary transits in order to select favorable time periods for new undertakings. It is continuously evolving, with additional factors being developed and refined to support such analysis.

2. Calendar and Day view
How to read the calendar?

The calendar displays, for each day of the month, Nakshatra, Tara Bala, Tithi, Karana, Nitya Yoga, and Chandra Bala as color-coded segments (time intervals) according to their favorability, as well as icons for lunar or solar eclipses and planetary transits.

What can be seen on the preview screen of a selected day?

This screen provides general information about Panchanga, Tara Bala, Chandra Bala, and planetary transits (according to the selected settings), as well as daily Muhurtas and VTN yogas. It is also possible to navigate forward or backward through the days of the month.

What are the main features of the day details screen?

This screen is the core of the application — a daily planner with a timeline and detailed visualization of all significant transits throughout the day. Each color segment provides a tooltip with detailed information. It is also possible to add personal notes (with a notification system).

3. Transit analysis
What is the transit mode?

A planetary transit refers to the movement of a planet through the signs of the Zodiac. There are three modes of displaying planetary transits: from the Moon, from the Lagna, and a combined mode (both simultaneously). If both modes are selected in the settings, the colored segment will be divided into two parts: the upper part represents the Moon-based transit, and the lower part represents the Lagna-based transit.

What information is displayed for monthly planetary transits?

You can see through which zodiac sign a planet is transiting and whether this transit is favorable (green color) or unfavorable (red color) by house from the natal Moon (and/or from Lagna) for the selected profile, as well as through which Nakshatra (with Tara Bala), Pada and Navamsa. Each Pada has its own color depending on favorability. Green marks a Pada that is Pushkara Navamsa. Red marks Gandanta. Pink marks Vish Navamsa and Drekkana (but if this Pada is also Pushkara Navamsa, the color becomes light green). Other Padas are light green. Next to the Pada number, after a dash, it is shown through which sign the planet goes in Navamsa, and if this is a sign of exaltation or debilitation, an upward or downward arrow is shown next to the Navamsa respectively. Now the strength of the planet in transit in D9 is clearly visible, as well as the time periods when it is Vargottama. Additionally, the planet’s transit through Mrityu Bhaga (fatal or dead degrees) is marked by default in orange.

Detailed information in the tooltip.

In the tooltip for each day (second tap), it is shown through which zodiac sign, Nakshatra (with Tara Bala) and Pada the planet is transiting, the time period of the transit, Navamsa, Vedha (from the natal Moon and from Lagna), and Mrityu Bhaga. For each Pada, it is separately indicated whether this Pada is Pushkara Navamsa, Gandanta, Ashtamamsa, Vargottama, Vish Navamsa, Sarpa, Nigada or Pasha Drekkana. It is also possible to track individual planetary transits through Malefic Navamsas (36th, 55th, 64th, 72nd, 81st, 88th, 96th) and Drekkanas (16th and 22nd) from the natal Moon and Lagna for the currently selected profile.

4. Colors and visual Indicators
What do the green and red colors mean?

Green indicates favorable conditions, while red indicates unfavorable ones. Different shades of green and red are also used to represent varying degrees of favorability.

Can I change the application colors?

Yes. The application includes a built-in editor for all used colors.

5. Notes, events and reminders
How to add a note or event?

To do this, open the details screen of the selected day — the daily planner page. By selecting any time segment on the timeline, a second tap allows you to open the notes editor.

How do reminders work?

All notes created for the selected profile are included in the notification system. By default, notifications are triggered 5 minutes in advance (this can be configured in settings).

Are reminders local or online?

Reminders work locally on the user's device.

6. Profiles and settings
Why do I need a profile?

The profile system is designed for convenience. Once saved, a profile allows you to avoid repeatedly entering the data required for calculations, such as date of birth, place of birth, place of residence, etc.

Can I use more than one profile?

Yes, you can create an unlimited number of profiles. For example, you can store data for the same person in multiple profiles, differing only in the place of residence.

What settings can be changed?

You can change the following settings: Language, First day of the week, Planetary transits, Nodes (Rahu, Ketu), Hora, 30 Muhurtas (and 60 Ghatis), Mrityu Bhaga, Sunrise, Notifications, and Color settings.

7. Privacy and support
Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device.

Is it mandatory to provide an email address?

No. This is an optional field in the feedback system. It is used for follow-up communication if you would like to receive a response.

How can I send feedback?

Feedback can be sent from the "About" screen.

How can I support the project?

You can support the development of the project from the "About" screen.
