PADMA – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 July 2026

1. Introduction

PADMA (Personal Astrological Diary Mobile App) is developed by Serhii Sheremet. This Privacy Policy explains how PADMA handles information when you use the application.

PADMA is designed as a personal diary and astrological calendar application. The app is intended to work primarily on your device and does not require a remote account or cloud synchronization.

2. Information We Handle

PADMA may handle the following information that you enter or configure in the app:

  • Profile data, such as a name or profile label
  • Birth details, such as date, time, place, time zone, and coordinates
  • Living location details, such as place, time zone, and coordinates
  • User notes, diary entries, events, reminders, and notification settings
  • App preferences, language settings, colors, and other configuration settings
  • Email address, only if you voluntarily provide it when submitting feedback or questions

3. Local Storage and On-Device Processing

Most PADMA data is stored locally on your device in the application database. PADMA does not maintain a remote database of your personal data.

Astrological calculations, diary data, profiles, events, and reminders are processed on your device.

4. How We Use Information

PADMA uses user-entered information only to provide app functionality, including:

  • Calculating and displaying astrological calendar data and transits
  • Showing personal diary entries, events, and reminders
  • Saving your app settings and preferences
  • Responding to feedback or questions if you voluntarily provide an email address

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Notifications

PADMA may use local notifications to remind you about your own events. These notifications are generated on your device and do not involve external servers.

6. Third-Party Services

PADMA may use standard platform services provided by Android, Google Play, and Apple system services for app distribution, installation, updates, crash handling, permissions, and operating system functionality.

PADMA does not use advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, cloud synchronization services, or third-party AI services.

Third-party platform services may process technical or diagnostic information according to their own privacy policies and system settings.

7. Data Sharing

PADMA does not share your locally stored personal diary, profile, event, reminder, or astrological data with the developer or with third parties.

If you choose to send feedback or contact the developer, the information you provide in that message, including your email address if included, will be used only to respond to your request.

8. Data Security

PADMA stores app data in the application’s local storage area on your device. Access to this data is controlled by the operating system’s standard app sandbox and device security mechanisms.

If any communication with the developer happens through a feedback or email mechanism, it should be transmitted using the security mechanisms provided by the relevant platform or email provider.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored in PADMA remains on your device until you delete it.

You can delete profiles, events, reminders, and other user-entered data inside the app where the relevant delete options are available. You can also remove PADMA data by uninstalling the app or clearing the app’s data through your device settings.

Please note that your operating system, device backup, or app store backup mechanisms may keep copies of app data according to your device and account settings.

If you contact the developer by email or feedback message, that communication may be retained as long as necessary to respond to your request and maintain support records.

10. Children

PADMA is not designed as an app directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Disclaimer

PADMA is intended for informational and personal use only. It does not provide professional, medical, financial, legal, or other professional advice.

12. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the feedback form in the application.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
